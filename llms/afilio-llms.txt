# Afilio

> Afilio (Afilio - Gesellschaft für Vorsorge mbH, Berlin) operates a consumer digital provision platform ("Vorsorgeplattform") that helps German families prepare for emergencies, care, and death. Members create legally valid advance directives (Patientenverfügung), healthcare powers of attorney (Vorsorgevollmacht), and wills (Testament), store a digital asset/document inventory, and share access through an emergency card that gives doctors and relatives 24/7 access to critical information. Over four million families use the GDPR-compliant service.

This is an API Evangelist network profile. Afilio is a business-to-consumer platform and does not currently publish a public developer API, OpenAPI specification, SDK, or developer portal. The links below point to Afilio's public product and legal surface.
